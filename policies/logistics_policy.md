# Logistics & Freight Rerouting Policy

**Document ID:** POL-LOG-002  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-06-01  
**Target Scope:** Fleet Management & Carrier Relations  

---

## 1. Expedite Shipping Constraints

1. **Cost Threshold:**
   - Expedited air/overnight freight can only be recommended if the value of delayed goods exceeds **$5,000** or affects a Tier-1 customer order.
   - Expedite cost surcharge must not exceed **$500 per shipment** without explicit VP approval.

2. **Carrier SLA & Risk Criteria:**
   - Carriers with historical late-delivery rates exceeding **15%** must be flagged as **HIGH_CARRIER_RISK**.
   - Shipments assigned to high-risk carriers during adverse weather (STORM/RAIN) must be rerouted to alternative vetted carriers.

---

## 2. Vehicle Routing & Capacity Rules

- **Vehicle Fill Rate:** Delivery routes generated by optimization algorithms must achieve a minimum vehicle load capacity fill rate of **75%**.
- **Max Driver Shift:** Route durations must not exceed **8 driving hours** or **500 km per shift** per vehicle.
